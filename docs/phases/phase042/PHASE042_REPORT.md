### What the switch does (behind `DAYNIGHT.enabled`)

- **Sky.** Each tick writes `sky_light`, `sky_night`, `sky_moon`, `sky_cloud`, `sky_sunrise`, `sky_sunset` on the town node. The same numbers are available as plain queries without the switch: `worlds.daynight.sky(world)`, `light_level(world)`, `is_night(world)`, `moon_illum(world)`. Programme E (Phases 062 and 065) can call `light_level(world)` or `view_light(world, actor)`.
- **Seeing.** `sees_dark(world, actor)`: outdoors (not inside any building footprint), no lit lamp within `lamp_reach` (1 cell), no lit candle held, and light below `see_threshold` (0.2). Indoors never.
- **Lamps.** Four `StreetLamp` nodes at the square's corners (offsets in `DAYNIGHT_LAMPS`). Each holds `lit`, `fuel` (capacity 4.0) and `burn_per_tick` (0.05). `light` on a lamp's cell fills the reservoir from the town's `oil` stock, then lights it. Lit lamps burn each tick; at zero fuel they go out. `douse` puts a lamp out and keeps its oil. Switch-on seeds 40 oil (`starting_oil`, APPROX), because the starting stock has none and oil is only pressed from linseed.
- **Candles.** `light` with a held `candle` lights it (fuel 1.0, burn 0.05 per tick, then spent). `douse` puts it out.
- **Lamplighter.** Switch-on appoints the first living adult (founding order) with the `lamplighter` job. The world does not check the role for `light` or `douse`. Only the simple backend's `lamplighter_choice` uses the job, and it is offered lamp options only when the lamplighter has a light or douse to do or a walk to a lamp that needs it. Other residents get none.
- **Dark work (Phase 048 fitness path).** The job's fitness is multiplied by `dark_work_fit` (0.3, APPROX) when the worker sees dark. Progress per tick, quality, the waste threshold (`waste_below` 0.25) and strain all follow the fitness, so a dark job is slower, loses quality, accrues strain and risks accidents. A bare-hand dark job falls below the waste threshold and wastes its inputs.
- **Sight.** A witness who sees dark has `sight_range` min(7, `dark_sight_range` 2, APPROX).
- **Travel.** Interval multiplied by `travel_dark_factor` (default 1.0, the seed) when the walker sees dark.
- **Lateness.** Phase 024's `lateness` is hours past the real sunset while it is night, 0 by day.
- **Words.** `now.light` in the agent view gives `in daylight`, `dim, in the moonlight`, `it is dark` or `by lamplight` (phrases with no digits). It is absent when the switch is off.
- **Self-test.** Cross-references must resolve when the switch is on, and may be empty when it is off.

## Limits

- **The lamplighter round does not work under the simple policy.** Lamps stay dark in the 60-turn and 200-tick runs. The switch therefore ships off.
- The **lantern is not added**. It needs an `ITEMS` row and art, and I did not regenerate assets. Candles are the carried light.
- **Lamp and lantern art** are not generated (follow-up).
- Visibility modulates **job progress only** (Phase 048's path). Attempts (Phase 050) and tool wear are not visibility-dependent.
- Sight uses the **observer's** `sees_dark`. The witnessed act's own lighting is not considered.
- Overcast daylight: the seed's light rule is `sun_light x (1 - cloud)`, so storm (cloud 0.9) by day gives 0.1, under `see_threshold` 0.2. The town reads dark in a storm by day. I kept the seed's rule.
- The sun: equation of time omitted, so sunrise and sunset are centred on `noon_hour` 12 (APPROX). Latitude 52 is an APPROX choice (the seed gives none). `summer_solstice_day` 135 maps the calendar's summer midpoint to the June solstice (APPROX).
- `new_moon_day` 0 is a choice (the founding clock begins at a new moon).
- The lamp state (`_dn_lamps`) is a plain world attribute and is not part of any checkpoint I checked. Persistence is untested.
- The lamp oil stock is a seeded 40 units, a design figure (APPROX). Oil is still consumed by bread and ink recipes, so lamps compete with cooking. The balance flow compares the stock with one period's burn; no production is modelled.
- The `light` numeric quantity in the lexicon (unpublished, from an earlier phase) is unchanged. The view uses the new status quantity `daylight`.
- Figures marked APPROX are design choices, not measured: `dark_work_fit`, `dark_sight_range`, `see_threshold`, `lamp_reach`, lamp capacity and burn, `starting_oil`, candle values, `travel_dark_factor` (1.0, the seed), the cloud table, the lamplighter's routine and group, and `JOB_PUBLIC`.

## Open questions

Each changes the simple policy, so it needs a decision before `DAYNIGHT.enabled` can default on.
2. Should **overcast daylight** keep the seed's `1 - cloud` rule (storm days read dark), or floor the daytime light?
3. Should **attempts (Phase 050)** also use the visibility factor?
4. Should the **lamplighter be drawn in the founding population** (a non-zero fixed load), or appointed on switch-on as now?
5. **Persistence:** lamps and the switch flag are not in `world_persistence` or `game_persistence`. Confirm which needs them.
6. Should the **lantern** be added (ITEMS row and art, a follow-up phase)?
