# Phase 047: joint actions, people acting together

## Summary

Several residents can now act as one. Two joint acts are built, behind the `joint_actions` switch (default off):

- **Carry.** A physical object on the ground that one adult cannot lift moves one cell per turn when the group's
  summed force times a coordination factor reaches its load. Short of the load, it stays put, and the carriers strain
  and spend stamina. A carrier who collapses, sleeps or leaves reach before the turn's tick ends drops the load.
- **Crew.** A recipe with a `crew` field {min, max} (`saw_planks`, 2 to 3) progresses faster with more workers on
  the worker's cell. A lone worker crawls at a penalty.

The world publishes a `joint` option to each resident who can take part, with `present` (the other residents who can
join now). Proposals with the same task key are resolved together by `BigvilleGame` before the turn's other responses.
The core's `team` term now takes its real value (summed force times `coordination(n)`). With one member the value is
1.0, so committed numbers are unchanged.

Switch off: no joint option is published, a joint proposal is refused as `unsupported`, and the crew field is ignored.
The 192-turn town probe gives 0 deaths before and after, and the 50-turn persona test passes before and after.

Not delivered from the brief: taking a stack from a holder with three people, gangs, group contests, and the dual-key
reading. See Limits.

Self-test: 0 errors, 0 warnings. Full suite: 1702 passed, 3 skipped, 7 failed. The 7 failures are not from this phase
(see Validation).

### Design

**Proposal shape.** A joint proposal is `ProposedAction("joint", {"joint": {...}})`. The `joint` object is
`{"task": "carry", "object": name, "toward": [dx, dy]}` or `{"task": "crew", "worker": name}`. The game groups
proposals by (task, object or worker, toward) and calls `world.enact_joint(task, members)`, which returns
`{resident: applied}`. No new major action is added, so the phase 054 packet snapshot is unchanged.

**Options.** `joint` options are published only with the switch on and only to residents who are alive, awake and
free:
- Carry: for each ground object within reach whose needs are at least 2. `needs` is the fewest people of the
  proposer's force who reach the load's demand, up to `max_members` (4). `toward` lists the steps onto cells that
  are walkable and hold no living resident.
- Crew: for each open crewed job whose worker stands on the resident's cell, with room for more. `needs` is
  `min` minus the workers present.

**Carry physics** (`_enact_carry`). The demand is mass over `load_unit` (50, the adult inventory capacity from
ACTOR_DEFAULTS). The core's `resolve_step` runs with force = summed member force, team = `coordination(n)`, and
intensity 1. `rho = capability / demand`. The load moves `floor(rho)` cells, capped at `pace_cap` (3), along `toward`,
stopping before any occupied cell. Carriers then stand one step behind the load. Each member pays its share of the
stamina and strain by force. A member's strain at the limit is an accident (Phase 050).

**Crew physics.** `crew_factor(members)` multiplies the worker's progress per tick. A lone worker gets `crew_penalty`
(0.25). Two or more get `members x coordination(members)`, with coordination lowered by the worker's recipe skill.
Each helper who joined pays stamina per tick in step with the worker's labour.

**Participation.** A resident who is asleep, collapsed, dead, on a timed job, out of reach, or whose slot is spent is
left out with a REFUSAL_REASONS code. The rest still act. A group short of `needed` still runs and fails physically.

**Observation.** Each act creates an event that awake residents within `sight_range` (7) observe. `last_joint` on the
resident's node records `joined`, `needed` and the outcome.

### Data and sources

All figures are APPROX model choices, unsourced unless stated.
- `load_unit` 50: the pack's own `ACTOR_DEFAULTS.inventory_max_load` (checked by the self-test).
- `max_members` 4, `pace_cap` 3, `reach` 1, `coordination_loss` 0.1, `skill_relief` 0.5, `crew_penalty` 0.25:
  model choices, tuned only so the test scenarios come out as described.
- `saw_planks` crew 2 to 3: pit sawing is classically a two-person job (a sawyer above, a pitman below). No source

## Changed numbers and behaviour

- Committed numbers: unchanged. With the switch off every crew factor is 1.0, and a lone resident's team term is
  `coordination(1) = 1.0`.
- The `joint_actions` switch is a new `physical_subsystems` key. Two existing tests asserted the whole dict (see
  below).
- Measured in the probes (not in the village):
  - Carry, two adults, 80-unit crate: capability 1.82 against demand 1.6, so rho 1.14 and one cell moved. One adult
    alone: capability 1.0, rho 0.625, unmoved, with strain 0.6 per attempt (by calculation), so a second attempt
    reaches the strain limit. The test checks that health falls within three attempts.
  - Carry, 40-unit crate: two adults move 2 cells (rho 2.27); four adults move 3, the cap (rho 3.85).
  - Crew, saw (2 ticks at full pace, 20 minutes): a lone worker progresses 0.25 per tick, so about 8 ticks. With one
    labourer helper it is 1.87 per tick (the helper's coordination is lowered by the sawyer's skill). With two helpers
    it is 2.63. The third worker adds 0.76, under one full worker's labour.

## Limits

- **Not built from the brief:**
  - Three children taking a stack from a smith (a group take). Phase 029's take has one attacker.
  - Gangs (simultaneous building labour and field work).
  - Group contests (several attackers, or several guards of a holder).
  - The dual-key reading. Phase 030's `keys_required` already exists and is not changed.
- **Oven.** The only item above one adult's capacity is the oven (200 weight, ITEMS). Four people give
  `4 x 0.769 = 3.08` against a demand of 4, so no joint carry of an oven is offered. The test uses an 80-unit crate.
- **Carry is per turn.** The group must propose again each turn. A load carried in one turn stays where it is if
  nobody proposes the next turn.
- **Carry strain and stamina** are split by force share (each member's force over the summed force). The brief's
  "Phase 029's share of its own force" is not used; the split is by contribution instead.
- **Crew helpers** pay stamina only, with no strain. They join once and keep contributing while they stand on the
  worker's cell, awake and free. A helper who leaves the cell stops contributing and is not removed from the list.
- **The `joint` proposal is not a major action.** It is not in `MAJOR_ACTIONS`, so the prompts (LLM, Ocelot) do not
  list it. An LLM or Ocelot resident sees only the affordance. The reply path was not checked against a live model,
  so whether an LLM's `joint` proposal is accepted is unknown.
- **`last_joint`** (joined k of N) is stored on the resident's node. It is not in the observations or the agent view.
- **The cheap rule** takes the first step the world lists (sorted, after removing occupied cells). Its direction is
  not chosen with intent, and it has no goal beyond the present count. It also proposes a carry whenever
  `present + 1 >= needs`, unless food or sleep comes first.
- **Turn order.** Joint acts resolve before the turn's other responses. This makes them independent of listing order,
  and it is a choice, not a rule the brief set.
- **Checkpoints.** The new state is plain values: `carriers` on a PhysicalObject, `crew` on a Job, `last_joint`, and
  Effort nodes. The other session's persistence code was not checked against these.
- **Not run:** a village or town run with the switch on. The switch-on behaviour is covered only by the probes and the
  32 tests.
